   - Paste FRONTEND variables in Frontend service
   - Click "Apply"

===========================================
CUSTOM DOMAIN (interprayerleague.com)
===========================================

After choosing Render or Railway as primary:

1. Add Domain:
   - Go to Frontend service → Settings → Custom Domains
   - Add: interprayerleague.com
   - Get DNS records (CNAME/A record)

2. Update Hostinger DNS:
   - Login: https://hpanel.hostinger.com/
   - Go to: Domains → interprayerleague.com → DNS
   - Add CNAME/A records provided by platform
   - Wait 1-48 hours for DNS propagation
